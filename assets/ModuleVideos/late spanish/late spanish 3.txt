Emilio Aguinaldo became an important leader of the Philippine Revolution through his success in battles, especially the victory at Imus. His leadership earned him respect, and he later became the first president of the revolutionary government and the Philippines. On June 12, 1898, Aguinaldo proclaimed Philippine independence in Kawit, Cavite, where the Philippine flag was first raised and the national anthem was played. In 1899, the Malolos Congress created the Malolos Constitution, forming Asia’s first democratic republic and showing Filipinos’ dream of self-rule.
