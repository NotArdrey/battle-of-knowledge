Andres Bonifacio, known as the Father of the Philippine Revolution, founded the Katipunan or KKK in 1892. It was a secret group that aimed to gain independence through armed struggle. Unlike Jose Rizal, Bonifacio believed fighting was necessary to end Spanish rule. The revolution began in 1896 with the Cry of Pugad Lawin, when members tore their cedulas to reject Spanish authority. As leader of the Katipunan, Bonifacio organized revolutionary forces across Luzon, showing the strong desire of Filipinos for freedom.
