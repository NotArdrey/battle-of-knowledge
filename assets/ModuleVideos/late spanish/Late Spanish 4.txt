Late Spanish 4

Apolinario Mabini was known as the “Brains of the Revolution” and served as Emilio Aguinaldo’s most trusted adviser. Even though he was paralyzed due to illness, his intelligence and leadership greatly helped the revolution. Mabini played a key role in writing the Malolos Constitution, which became the foundation of the First Philippine Republic. As the first Prime Minister, he believed in true independence and warned Filipinos against replacing one foreign ruler with another, emphasizing the need for a government that truly served the Filipino people.
