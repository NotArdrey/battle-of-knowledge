Dr. Jose Rizal was a Filipino writer, doctor, and reformist who fought Spanish abuse through peaceful means. He studied in Europe and used his education to speak out against injustice. His novels Noli Me Tangere and El Filibusterismo exposed the cruelty and corruption of Spanish rule and awakened Filipino nationalism. Rizal also founded La Liga Filipina to promote peaceful reforms. His execution on December 30, 1896, made him a national hero and inspired Filipinos to continue the fight for freedom.
