On March 16, 1521, Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan, sailing under the Spanish flag, arrived in the Philippines while seeking a westward route to the valuable Spice Islands.

The journey unfolded through Magellan first landing in to Cebu by March 28. On April 14, 1521, Raja Humabon with his followers converted to Christianity in what would become the first Catholic mass in the Philippines. This religious conversion set the stage for the historic Battle of Mactan that followed on April 27.
