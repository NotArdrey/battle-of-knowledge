The Battle of Mactan, on April 27, 1521, marked a pivotal moment in Philippine history. It began when Ferdinand Magellan demanded submission from Lapu-Lapu. Magellan led a small Spanish force against a much larger group of Filipino warriors. Although the Spaniards had superior weapons and armor, they struggled in the shallow waters and unfamiliar terrain. Using their knowledge of the land and strong unity, Lapu-Lapu’s forces overwhelmed the invaders, defending their independence and traditional way of life.
