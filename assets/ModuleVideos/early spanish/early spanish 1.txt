Before 1521, the Philippines was made up of independent communities called barangays, each led by a datu or chieftain. Filipinos has already developed their own writing system called baybayin and maintained active trade relationships with China, Japan, and other Asian neighbors. Society was organized into distinct classes including nobles (maharlika), freemen, and the slaves. While traditional religious practices centered around animism, while some communities in the south had already embraced Islamic influences brought by traders from neighboring regions.
